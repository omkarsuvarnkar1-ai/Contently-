## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of creative fluidity and analytical precision. Built for modern content creators, growth marketers, and editorial teams orchestrating high-velocity digital output, the interface radiates intelligence, clarity, and momentum.

The design movement combines **Modern SaaS Minimalism** with subtle **Bioluminescent Accents**—a clean architectural structure anchored by crisp whites and slate neutrals, punctuated by energetic shifts of electric indigo, cyan, and soft violet. The aesthetic communicates effortless competence: layouts breathe with generous negative space, surfaces feel crystalline yet substantial, and dynamic AI-powered interactions are distinguished through fine, glowing gradient treatments rather than heavy visual noise. 

The emotional response evoked is focused empowerment: the quiet confidence of a pristine creative studio augmented by invisible, hyper-competent machine intelligence.

## Colors

The palette leverages a cool-tinted, hyper-clean foundation allowing vibrant spectral gradients to signal agency, state change, and automated synthesis.

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` Electric Indigo):** The grounding interactive hue. Applied to standard key actions, primary indicators, focused input rings, and foundational navigation anchors.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` Cyan):** The velocity accent. Paired in tandem with the primary color to generate directional energy, indicate automated workflows, and power active progress states.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` Soft Violet):** The generative tone. Reserved for generative prompts, AI copilot trigger states, token/usage highlights, and contextual suggestions.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` Slate Gray):** The structural balancer. Scaled down to `#0F172A` for high-legibility primary typography, `#64748B` for metadata and de-emphasized body text, `#E2E8F0` for perimeter structural delineation, and `#F8FAFC` as the clean canvas base.
- **Semantic Feedback:** Precision alerts utilize `#10B981` (Success) for asset synchronization, schedule confirmation, and approval states; and `#EF4444` (Error) for validation dropouts and quota breaches.

### The Luminescence Gradient
A signature linear angle of `135deg`, blending `#4F46E5` smoothly through `#8B5CF6` to `#06B6D4`, is reserved strictly for high-tier touchpoints: primary execution buttons ("Generate", "Publish"), active AI workspace badges, and interactive gradient ring borders during computational states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all primary layers. Its geometric foundation combined with open apertures delivers immediate contemporary authority while sustaining superior legibility across dense data tables, editorial document editors, and analytics cards.

### Typographic Rules
- **Headlines:** Display and H1–H3 tiers feature tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and bold weights (`700` and `800`) to create authoritative visual stopping power against crisp, white backdrops.
- **Body Rhythm:** Default copy sits at a deliberate `500` (Medium) weight rather than `400` (Regular) at standard scale. This reinforces contrast against bright surfaces without needing to rely on pure black values, eliminating optical glare.
- **Functional Labels & Metadata:** Navigation links, status pills, and tab indicators make strict use of semi-bold or bold tracking-expanded uppercase/title case to clarify interactive context.
- **Technical Content:** `JetBrains Mono` handles inline variable injections, prompt templates, and schema key displays within the generative editor panel.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an **adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid** anchored by a structured rhythm system predicated on 8px base units (with occasional 4px subdivisions for dense toolbars).

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width constrained to `1600px` for multi-pane editorial layouts, fluid column gutters of `1.5rem` to `2rem`, and outer page canvas margins of `2.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, collateral sidebars fold into collapsible canvas slide-overs, `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column stack layout, edge margins drop to `1rem`, nested gutters reduce to `1rem`.

### Workspace Spatial Hierarchy
To prevent cognitive overload within an AI-assisted orchestration tool:
- Macro workspace regions (e.g., Kanban boards, dynamic split-screen text editor vs. AI chat assistant) maintain minimum outer gaps of `space-xl` (`2rem`).
- Internal module padding within panels and canvas cards scales smoothly between `space-md` (`1rem`) on compact views and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on expanded cards.
- Interactive groupings (e.g., input field paired with descriptive label or button icon clusters) strictly adhere to `space-xs` (`0.25rem`) and `space-sm` (`0.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of **micro-borders** and **multi-tiered ambient shadows** tinted with indigo, replacing dark muddy drops with luminous, optical depth.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** The structural stage. Holds the workspace frames, rail bars, and secondary gutter zones.
- **Surface Elevation 01 (`#FFFFFF`):** Base cards, data tables, and input containers. Bounded by a crisp, continuous outline: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Elevation 02 (Interactive Cards & Popovers):** Active content tiles, dropdown menus, and hover-triggered cards. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Outlines maintain `#E2E8F0`.
- **Surface Elevation 03 (Floating Dialogs, AI Toolbars, & Modals):** Top-level generation prompts, command palettes (Cmd+K), and asset modals. Features subtle frosted glass styling: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Shadow: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`. Border shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.

### State Elevations
Hovering over actionable cards does not scale the element; rather, it transitions the border color from `#E2E8F0` to `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.3)` while deepening the indigo ambient blur.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an open, friendly, yet rigorous shape geometry characterized by generous curvature across panels balanced with precision radii for utility elements.

- **Workspace Cards & Major Surfaces:** Scaled to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) or `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`), softening the structural architecture and highlighting visual warmth.
- **Controls & Form Fields:** Standard inputs, buttons, and segmented pickers adopt `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to maintain clean alignment and sharp form edges.
- **Chips, Pills, & AI Status Indicators:** Retain fully circular border radii (`9999px`), creating immediate visual distinction from rectangular structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gradient CTA:** Reserved for primary workspace triggers (e.g., "Generate Content", "Schedule Campaign"). Background: `linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5 0%, #8B5CF6 50%, #06B6D4 100%)`. Typography: `#FFFFFF`, `label-md`. Shadow: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.25)`. Transitions on hover to an expanded shadow with `1.01` slight scale shift.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text color `#0F172A`. Transitions on hover to background `#F8FAFC` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FEE2E2`, text `#EF4444`. Hover switches to `#EF4444` with white text.

### Chips & Badges
- **AI Context Badges:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), with a `1px` gradient border frame and an interior fill of `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.9)`. Text uses `#4F46E5` accompanied by an inline sparkling spark icon (12px).
- **Status Pills:** Background `#F1F5F9`, `#0F172A` text for Drafts; background `#ECFDF5`, `#10B981` text for Published; background `#EFF6FF`, `#06B6D4` text for Scheduled.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- Height: `42px` standard for inputs; multiline generous padding for copy editors.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Border radius: `8px`.
- Typography: `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus State: Border color switches directly to `#4F46E5` accompanied by an ambient focus ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Content Panels
- Structural background of `#FFFFFF` wrapped with a uniform `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border and `20px` corner radii.
- Content card headers feature a distinct divider border (`border-b: 1px solid #F1F5F9`), housing title hierarchy (`headline-sm`) along with metadata and action kebab triggers.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision `18px x 18px` controls with an inactive border of `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` against an `#FFFFFF` background.
- Active state fills with `#4F46E5` (Checkmark icon in crisp `#FFFFFF`), surrounded by a subtle `0 0 0 2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.1)` outline.

### AI Prompt Bar (Specialized Component)
- Docked or floating pill-shaped or high-radii surface (`16px`) using Surface Elevation 03.
- Features a dynamic gradient ring border (`linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5, #06B6D4)`) that shifts opacity based on user focus and computation activity.
- Integrated quick-action tags (e.g., "Rewrite", "Shorten", "Optimize SEO") surfaced as small white buttons inside the prompt interface.
